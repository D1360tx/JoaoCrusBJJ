What grows here

SMALL WINS.
BIG SKILLS.

We use movement and simple games to practice the abilities parents want to see beyond the mat. Progress looks like trying again, waiting one more turn, naming a feeling, or finding the courage to join.

01

Confidence

Not a participation trophy. A real memory of facing something new and discovering, “I can do this.”

02

Focus & discipline

Listen for one instruction, move with purpose, stop safely, and practice the rhythm again.

03

Social skills

Share space, take turns, set a safe boundary, respect a partner, and become part of the group.

TODDLER RHYTHM.

A long lecture will lose a three-year-old. So we do the opposite: short explanations, active practice, and clear transitions that make the class feel achievable.

No “perfect kid” required

THEY DON'T HAVE TO
BE READY-MADE.

The program is for children who are still learning the basics of being in a group. Those are not reasons to wait. They are the reasons to begin carefully.

Not expected on day one

  • Perfect listening
  • Knowing how to sit still
  • Being fearless or outgoing
  • Any martial arts experience

What we help grow

  • Trying after frustration
  • Following one clear direction
  • Safe body control
  • Confidence around new people

Parent questions

ASK THE
REAL STUFF.

Three-year-olds are three-year-olds. You do not need to pretend otherwise before reaching out.

Is my child really old enough?

The class is intentionally built around short activities, games, movement, and repetition. We start at age 3 because the format meets children where they are.

What if they are shy or won't join immediately?

That is okay. A first visit can include watching, warming up slowly, and joining one piece at a time. We are looking for a safe first step, not a performance.

Do parents stay?

Yes. Parents watch from the side, so your child can see you and you can see how the class is taught.

What should they wear?

Comfortable clothes they can move in are fine for the first visit. We will explain uniform options if you decide to continue.
