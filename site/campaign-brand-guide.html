Foundation

WHAT THE BRAND
SHOULD FEEL LIKE.

A strong campaign poster brought to life. The system balances athletic energy with Joao's experience, emotional intelligence, and beginner-friendly teaching.

Always communicate

  • Confidence earned through practice
  • Calm and control under pressure
  • Safe boundaries and respect
  • Belonging for beginners and families
  • 25+ years of teaching authority

Never drift into

  • Generic MMA aggression
  • Luxury bronze and crimson styling
  • Soft daycare or preschool clichés
  • Rounded SaaS cards and glass effects
  • Unverified promises or medical claims

Photography

SHOW THE ACADEMY.

Real community proof is part of the brand, not decoration. Photography should show teaching, belonging, movement, and emotional safety.

Production rules

  • Use real Joao Crus BJJ people and spaces
  • Keep faces and academy identity inside the crop
  • Show connection, movement, and teaching
  • Preserve natural skin tone and gi color
  • Optimize owned assets to WebP
  • Verify desktop and 390px mobile crops
Voice and messaging

SPECIFIC BEATS
SPECTACULAR.

Joao's authority is strongest when the copy explains what happens, why it matters, and how a first step works.

Write this way

Direct, experienced, reassuring, parent-aware, and grounded in observable practice.

  • "Small challenges give them room to try again."
  • "Parents stay and watch."
  • "Plan a first class."
  • "Research on preschool movement programs suggests..."

Avoid this drift

Generic hype, combat-first promises, medical overreach, or details that have not been confirmed.

  • "Unleash your child's inner warrior."
  • "The number one academy for everyone."
  • "BJJ is medically proven at age three."
  • "Learn more" when a specific CTA is available.
Accessibility

ENERGY WITHOUT
EXCLUSION.

The campaign look depends on contrast and structure. Accessibility strengthens the design rather than softening it.

Visual and interaction

  • Normal text must meet WCAG AA contrast
  • Focus rings must remain clearly visible
  • Tap targets should be at least 44px by 44px
  • Information cannot exist only on hover
  • Reduced-motion preferences must be respected

Structure and content

  • Use one H1 and preserve heading order
  • Include a skip link and semantic landmarks
  • Use visible labels on every form field
  • Write meaningful alt text for informative images
  • Keep sticky CTAs away from forms and schedule controls
