Questions parents ask

WHAT IF MY CHILD ISN'T “READY”?

Is 3 really old enough?

For this class, yes. The activities are short, active, and designed for this stage. Your child does not need perfect listening skills before arriving—that is one of the skills we practice.

My child is shy, busy, or slow to warm up. Is that okay?

Yes. New children can watch, stay close, and join at their own pace. Confidence grows from safe, manageable participation—not pressure to perform immediately.

Do parents stay?

Yes. Parents watch from the side so your child can see you and you can see exactly how the class works.

What should they wear?

Comfortable clothes they can move in are fine for the first visit. Uniform details can be discussed after you choose to continue.
