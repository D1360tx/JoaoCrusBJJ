WHY PRIVATE COACHING

MORE MOVES
ARE NOT
THE ANSWER.

Group classes give you breadth. Rolling gives you feedback. Private coaching can connect the two by slowing down the exact problem that keeps appearing in your game.

The goal is not to collect more moves. It is to leave knowing what matters, why it matters, and what to train next.

PRIVATE COACHING FAQ

KNOW WHAT HAPPENS NEXT.

Do I need prior jiu-jitsu experience?

No specific experience level is assumed in this preview. The fit quiz starts by learning where you are now so the conversation can match your situation.

Does private coaching replace group class?

It can complement group training by giving you a personal focus to carry into regular classes and open mat.

Can we work on one specific position?

Yes. A recurring position or decision can be a useful place to start, especially when it connects to a problem you keep seeing in live training.

How many sessions will I need?

The first conversation should determine whether one assessment, a short focused series, or ongoing coaching makes the most sense. No package claim is made in this review build.

What happens after the quiz?

In production, your answers can give Joao's team context before they follow up. This review version shows the experience but does not send or store any information.
