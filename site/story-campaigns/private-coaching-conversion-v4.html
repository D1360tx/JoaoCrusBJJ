PRIVATE COACHING SHOULD FIT THE PERSON

FOR ADULTS WITHLIMITED MAT TIME.

Private coaching is most useful when the session is attached to a real problem and a clear next use.

THIS CAN FIT YOU IF...

  • You keep reaching the same dead end in live training.
  • You know techniques, but they do not yet connect into a game.
  • You are returning after time away and need a focused re-entry.
  • Your schedule makes every available class or open mat matter.
  • You have a competition, position, or deadline to prepare for.

THIS MAY NOT FIT YOU IF...

  • You only want a large package before testing the coaching fit.
  • You want more random techniques without applying the current priority.
  • You are looking for a shortcut that replaces regular practice.
  • You are not ready to describe the pattern, test a correction, and bring feedback back.

PRIVATE COACHING FAQ

KNOW WHAT HAPPENSBEFORE YOU BEGIN.

What if I do not know what I need to work on?

You do not need a technical diagnosis. Start with what you notice: a position you repeatedly lose, a point where your options disappear, or a goal that is not connecting to your training. The assessment can help identify the priority.

Is a private useful if I am still new?

Experience level is only part of the decision. A focused assessment can determine whether private coaching would add useful personal feedback now or whether regular classes should remain the priority.

Does private coaching replace group class?

It can complement group training by giving you a personal focus to carry into regular classes and open mat.

Can we work on one specific position?

Yes. A recurring position or decision can be a useful place to start, especially when it connects to a problem you keep seeing in live training.

Can I share a roll clip?

A short clip can give useful context when you have one. It is optional. You can also begin with a repeated position, outcome, or question you notice in training.

How many sessions will I need?

Start with the problem, not a package. The first conversation should determine whether one assessment, a short focused series, or ongoing coaching makes the most sense.
