PRIVATE COACHING FAQ

KNOW WHAT HAPPENS NEXT.

What if I do not know what I need to work on?

You do not need a technical diagnosis. Start with what you notice: a position you repeatedly lose, a point where your options disappear, or a goal you cannot yet connect to your training. The assessment can help identify the priority.

Is a private worth it if I am still new?

Experience level is only part of the decision. A focused assessment can determine whether private coaching would add useful personal feedback now or whether regular classes should remain the priority.

Does private coaching replace group class?

It can complement group training by giving you a personal focus to carry into regular classes and open mat.

Can we work on one specific position?

Yes. A recurring position or decision can be a useful place to start, especially when it connects to a problem you keep seeing in live training.

How many sessions will I need?

Start with the problem, not a package. The first conversation should determine whether one assessment, a short focused series, or ongoing coaching makes the most sense. No package claim is made in this review build.

What happens after the quiz?

In production, your answers can give Joao's team context before they follow up. This review version shows the experience but does not send or store any information.
