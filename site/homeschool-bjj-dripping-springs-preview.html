More than solo exercise

LEARN BESIDE OTHER CHILDREN.

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu gives children a real person to learn with. Progress depends on paying attention, adjusting, and beginning again when something does not work yet.

1

Take a turn. Practice both sides of a shared activity.

2

Notice a partner. Learn that pace and position affect another person.

3

Reset and try again. Treat mistakes as information, not the end of the lesson.

Questions from homeschool parents

START WITH THE BASICS.

What ages can join the homeschool BJJ program?

The published homeschool program is for children ages 5–8.

When does the class meet?

The class meets Tuesday and Thursday from 10:30–11:15 AM.

Where is the homeschool program?

Classes are at the Dripping Springs academy, 120 Frog Pond Lane, Suite 200, Dripping Springs, TX 78620.

Does my child need Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu experience?

No prior martial arts experience is needed to ask about a first visit. The academy can help confirm whether this age group and class are the right fit.

What happens after I send a request?

The production form will send the request to Joao Crus BJJ so the academy can follow up about fit, availability, and the next appropriate step. This review form does not transmit information.
