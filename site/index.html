Why we train differently

Most schools teach you to win.
We teach you to regulate.

Jiu-Jitsu is the tool. The real work is who you become through it — someone who knows how to stay present when things get hard, set boundaries that protect their peace, and move through conflict without losing themselves.

For Kids

They learn they can handle hard things.

On the mat, children meet real pressure in a safe place — and discover they can stay calm, persist, and name what they feel. Discipline through practice. Focus because the work demands it. Confidence that's earned, not handed out.

For Adults

Emotional regulation under pressure.

Trapped in side control and every instinct says panic — we teach you to breathe and respond. Frames and boundaries on the mat become the same skills you use in every hard conversation and overwhelming moment off it.

On the mat & beyond

Find your starting line.

All levels welcome — no experience, no fitness required. Most people walk in stressed and leave grounded. That's not a tagline; it's what students tell us every week.

The man behind the method

A rare pedigree,
a deeper mission.

Joao Crus came up under legends — earning his path through Carlson Gracie, who he first befriended, then trained under, alongside the legendary Ricardo De La Riva, whose guard bears his name. But what sets Joao apart isn't only where he learned. It's what he chose to do with it.

  • Founded Joao Crus BJJ in 2003 — two decades serving Dripping Springs, with students from the early years now bringing their own children to the mat.
  • ✦Author of Grapple with Emotions & From Trauma to Triumph; host of the Black Belt Parenting Life podcast.
  • ✦A recognized authority in teaching children — top coaches and school owners across the U.S. and overseas turn to him to build their own kids' programs.
  • ✦Montessori-informed method. Speaks four languages. Has taught seminars across Europe.
