The Method
We don't just teach you to win. We teach you to regulate.

On the mat, you can't fake a boundary. BJJ becomes the safest place to feel big feelings, set limits, and stay calm under pressure. Our Montessori-informed approach turns grappling into a tool for emotional regulation, boundary-setting, and confidence — for the smallest kids to the busiest adults. Come in stressed, leave grounded.

Relational First

Connection before correction. We build trust, then technique — every belt earned through real relationship.

Physical Second

World-class grappling and self-defense, taught with patience. No experience or fitness required to start.

Regulation Over Reaction

Big feelings learn brave moves. Students leave able to name emotions, hold boundaries, and stay calm under pressure.

The Pedigree

A Lineage You Can Feel

Joao Crus is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt who trained, befriended, and then instructed under the legendary Carlson Gracie. He was further coached by Ricardo de la Riva — yes, the De La Riva guard is named after him — and by Marcello Monteiro.

Over 25+ years he has guided 500+ families, authored two books, hosts a podcast, speaks four languages, and teaches a Montessori-informed method that coaches and school owners across the US and overseas seek out for advice.
