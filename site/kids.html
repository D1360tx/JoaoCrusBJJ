Two stages, built for two very different minds

Right-sized for who they are now.

Ages 3–7

Little Champions

Where big feelings learn brave moves. Every class is built around emotional readiness first — before a single technique, we teach your child to listen to their body, manage frustration, and feel proud of small wins.

  • ✦Confidence through play — structured games that build courage without pressure.
  • ✦Emotional vocabulary — naming and regulating feelings on and off the mat.
  • ✦Safe boundaries — personal space, and how to say "stop" with clarity.
Ages 8–12

Junior Warriors

Train hard. Think deeper. Rise stronger. This is where kids stop just playing a sport and start discovering who they are — right when peer pressure, school stress, and self-doubt show up hard.

  • ✦Real technique, real progress — a structured belt curriculum with milestones to chase.
  • ✦Resilience under pressure — controlled sparring teaches calm when things get hard.
  • ✦Leadership by example — older kids mentor newer students.

"My kid walks differently now." — that's what 25 years of teaching this age looks like.
