Meet your coach

JIU-JITSU WITH A REASONBEHIND EVERY MOVE.

Joao Crus has taught Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu since founding his academy in 2003. His approach is shaped by the Carlson Gracie and Ricardo De La Riva lineages, not as names on a wall, but as a living emphasis on pressure, position, timing, efficiency, and control.

For children, that means purposeful activities matched to how they learn. For adults, it means technical coaching without competition-gym posturing. For private students, it means a game built around the person in front of him.
