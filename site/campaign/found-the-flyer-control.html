The real lesson

THE MOVE MATTERS. THE RESPONSE MATTERS MORE.

A partner resists. A position stops working. Frustration shows up. Students learn to pause, protect themselves, solve the next problem, and try again.

That practice looks different for a 3-year-old and an adult beginner. The lesson is the same: pressure does not have to decide what you do next.

Jiu-Jitsu is the practice. A more capable response is what students carry with them.

Why Joao teaches this way

CONTROL THE POSITION. KEEP CONTROL OF YOURSELF.

Joao Crus has taught Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu since founding his academy in 2003. The Carlson Gracie and Ricardo De La Riva lineages live in his teaching through pressure, position, timing, efficiency, and control.

But control is not domination. It is knowing when to move, when to pause, how to protect yourself, and how to respect the person in front of you. Children practice it through age-aware activities. Adults practice it through technical problems that meet them where they are.
