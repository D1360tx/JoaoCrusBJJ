A different kind of Jiu-Jitsu class

BUILT AROUND LEARNING, NOT PROVING YOURSELF.

This program is not designed around aggressive sparring, competition, or keeping up with younger athletes. We use Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu as a practical way to move, think, and learn with other people.

Move with more awarenessMobility, balance, coordination, and comfortable movement on the mat.
Practice useful physical skillsBody awareness, changing positions, and getting up and down from the floor.
Stay calm while solving problemsBreathing, simple techniques, and controlled positional games.
Learn with a partnerCooperative exercises, gradual resistance, and clear communication.
Build confidence through practiceNo pressure to perform at anyone else's pace.
Connect with a communityKeep learning, laughing, and building trust with a small group.
What a typical class looks like

FIVE PARTS. ONE STEADY FLOW.

Each session begins gently, builds toward simple partner practice, and closes with recovery, questions, and conversation.

  1. 01

    Mobility, balance, and warm-up

    Gentle movement, breathing, coordination, and time to become comfortable on the mat.

  2. 02

    Jiu-Jitsu movement skills

    Practice moving efficiently, changing positions, maintaining balance, and getting up and down from the floor.

  3. 03

    Technique and partner practice

    Learn simple Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu concepts slowly and practice them with a cooperative partner.

  4. 04

    Controlled positional games

    Use light problem-solving exercises to apply what you learned without aggressive sparring.

  5. 05

    Cool down and conversation

    Breathing, recovery, questions, and time to connect with the group.

No aggressive sparring

THE GOAL IS NOT TO WIN A ROUND.

The goal is to keep learning and leave class feeling better than when you arrived.

Traditional Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu often includes live sparring. This introductory program takes a different approach. Students first learn how to move, think, and work with another person safely.

  • No expectation to fight, compete, or prove anything
  • Cooperative practice is the foundation
  • Resistance is introduced gradually and only when appropriate
  • Every movement can be adapted to the individual
Teaching philosophyRELATIONAL FIRST.
PHYSICAL SECOND.

We create the relationship and sense of safety first. Then we teach the physical skills.

Who this is for

CURIOUS IS QUALIFIED.

This four-week introduction is for men and women age 60 and over who want a supportive way to stay active, keep learning, and try Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu.

  • Complete beginners and people who have never practiced martial arts
  • Adults interested in mobility, balance, and coordination practice
  • People who want a new physical and mental challenge
  • Anyone uncomfortable with a traditional fight-gym environment
  • People looking for connection and a supportive community
4-week introduction
