A living lineage

TRAINED IN TRADITION. BUILT FOR TODAY.

Dripping Springs · December 2005 · Original seminar footage

Joao was born in Brazil and trained there with Carlson Gracie, carrying a direct connection to one of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu’s most influential teams.

Within the Gracie tradition, Carlson became known for a proactive, pressure-oriented approach and a team culture that tested technique honestly. Joao brings that standard into a different kind of academy: serious training governed by responsibility, awareness, and care for the person.

The idea

A WARRIOR IN A GARDEN.

Build real capability so strength is available when needed, with enough judgment that it never has to control you.
