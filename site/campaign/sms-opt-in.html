To contact Joao Crus BJJ about your request, scheduling, or class information, use our contact form or a first-class request popup on the academy website. Enter your contact details and separately select either optional SMS checkbox only if you want that message category. One checkbox covers recurring automated non-promotional customer-care messages about your request, scheduling, and class information. The other covers recurring automated promotional messages about academy programs, offers, and events. The choices are independent and unchecked by default.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. You can submit your request without selecting either SMS checkbox.
