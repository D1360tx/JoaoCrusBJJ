Questions before you begin

What the finder does, and what happens next.

Do I need previous experience?

No. Completely new, returning, and currently training students can use the finder.

Will it recommend a current program?

Yes. The logic prioritizes factual age, location, format, and current confirmed availability. It does not invent an Austin adult group schedule.

Does the quiz book a class?

No. The finder sends your answers and contact request securely to Joao Crus BJJ, then shows your likely starting point. A team member confirms current availability and scheduling.

How long does it take?

Most visitors can answer the six questions in about 60 seconds.
