- translate “warrior in a garden” into capability governed by judgment;
- embed the montage and link to the expanded About story.

### About Joao

Place the expanded section after Joao's method/bio and before the coaching team. Organize the teaching inheritance as:

- Carlson Gracie — pressure reveals;

## Messaging guardrails

- Do not frame Carlson's history as proof that one Gracie branch is superior.
- Do not use feud, humiliation, or “smashing” language for family audiences.
- Do not present “warrior in a garden” as a direct Carlson quotation.
- Use historical specificity and original footage for prestige; avoid exaggerated adjectives.
- Keep “final seminar” out of public page copy until Joao confirms it.
