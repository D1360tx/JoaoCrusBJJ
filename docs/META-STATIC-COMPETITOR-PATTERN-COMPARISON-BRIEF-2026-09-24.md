## 2. Offer, audience and landing-page contract

- Dripping Springs concepts are Youth ages 8-12, marketed to adults/parents, not children. Destination: `https://joaocrusbjj.com/kids-first-class/`, child finder, contact capture last.
- Austin A and C are adult concepts; B is Youth ages 8-12. Destination for all three: `https://joaocrusbjj.com/castle-hill-grand-opening/`, combined child/adult choice without a preselected branch. Location: inside Castle Hill Fitness, 1112 N Lamar Blvd, Austin.
- Approved proposition: a free studio visit, with observation or participation. Joao calls to discuss fit and arrange a visit for leads who do not self-schedule. No price, free uniform, unlimited pass, deadline, scarcity or promised result is added. Platform CTA is **LEARN_MORE** in every concept.
- Important dated nuance: CURRENT-DECISIONS September 14/22 permits optional matched self-scheduling *after* successful lead capture. The lead form itself does not reserve a slot. The new ads emphasize the callback route; they must not be read as asserting that optional scheduling does not exist. No instant-booking promise is used.
- Schedules are intentionally omitted to reduce unverified/stale detail. No opening date or “coming soon” claim. The historical Castle Hill URL slug is not a newly confirmed opening.
- Landing-page match is **source-document match**, not fresh live QA. Before traffic, inspect current mobile destinations, visible opening-date copy, correct program choice, accepted lead behavior, callback/self-schedule wording, consent and attribution. Do not award a verified-live score based on these URLs alone.

## 5. Side-by-side evaluation rubric

Use the same seven scores for each concept, 1 to 5. Do not convert these judgment scores into predicted CTR, CPA or revenue. Keep factual/permission blockers separate from aesthetic preference.

| Dimension | 1: weak | 3: workable | 5: strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Message clarity | Offer/program unclear | Understandable after reading | Place, audience and next step obvious immediately |
| Local relevance | Generic or wrong locality | Correct city | Distinct place/program fit with no false scene-location inference |
| Visual proof | Misleading photo claim | Relevant but limited evidence | Real photo clearly supports the stated claim within its limits |
| Mobile readability | Clipping, tiny core text, busy | Readable with effort | Hook/CTA legible at phone scale in both formats |
| Policy/claim safety | Unsupported promise or privacy inference | Mostly cautious with a revision | No invented outcome, chronology, instant booking, personal diagnosis or misrepresented scene |
| Landing-page match | Wrong program/offer/route | Documented match; live QA outstanding | Offer and route are aligned, with evidence label explicit; no unverified live claims |
| Test distinctness | Cosmetic duplicate | Some hypothesis difference | Clearly different reason to respond, with remaining confounds acknowledged |

**Hard vetoes override any total:** uncleared paid-media rights, wrong age/location, implied tap/reset sequence, invented friendship or coaching proof, unapproved offer, opening-date claim, broken/mismatched destination. All current concepts remain rights/approval-gated even if copy safety scores well. A safe wording score is not a deployment clearance score.

Use [comparison-sheet.csv](../assets/meta/competitor-pattern-static-wave-1/comparison-sheet.csv) for independent reviewer scores and exact revisions. All score cells are blank intentionally; no Claude evaluation has been performed. Compare A/B/C within DS first. For Austin, A and C are adult while B is youth: rank for creative promise, but flag audience/photo confounding. It is not a clean causal three-way A/B test. Formats are derivatives, not additional hypotheses.

**After selection:** refine one DS and one Austin concept, approve exact copy, clear rights, validate current destinations, then seek separate upload permission. Do not add all six to the budget by default or replace the selected workroom controls. Later measurement should follow qualified local conversations, attended visits and enrollments, not longevity or CTR alone. Actual test allocation, stop-loss and attribution need approval.
