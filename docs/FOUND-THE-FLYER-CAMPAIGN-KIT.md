# Found the Flyer Campaign Kit

**Status:** internal review, not live
**Audience:** people who found a Joao Crus BJJ flyer or postcard on a parked car or at a local partner
**Primary action:** scan the QR code, watch Joao's short welcome, request the right first class
**Campaign URL:** `https://joaocrusbjj.com/found-the-flyer/`

## Campaign idea

The physical card and landing page behave like one conversation.

1. The card interrupts with: **“Don't throw this away yet.”**
2. The reverse earns the scan with: **“Now see why this flyer found you.”**
3. Joao acknowledges the exact behavior in the opening video: **“You didn't throw the flyer away.”**
4. The page turns general curiosity into one of three paths: child, adult, or help me choose.
5. The form captures a request for a matched first class rather than forcing an unconfirmed offer or price.

This preserves the result Joao has already seen from vehicle flyers while adding message match, attribution, a human welcome, and a measurable conversion path.

## 45-second primary video script

**Target runtime:** 40–50 seconds
**Format:** vertical 9:16 master, with a center-safe 4:5 and 16:9 crop
**Delivery:** warm, amused, direct, not salesy

> **[0:00–0:05 · Joao to camera]**
> “Great. You didn’t throw the flyer away.”
>
> **[0:05–0:10 · small smile]**
> “That means Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu has your attention, at least a little.”
>
> **[0:10–0:22 · cut between Joao and real class footage]**
> “I’m Joao Crus. I’ve been teaching Jiu-Jitsu since 2003, from children starting at age three to adults who are walking onto the mat for the very first time.”
>
> **[0:22–0:34 · coaching details, tapping, movement, adult instruction]**
> “You do not need to get in shape first. You do not need to know what a gi is. You just need the right first class, with a coach who knows where to start.”
>
> **[0:34–0:47 · Joao to camera, point or glance toward on-screen button]**
> “Tap the button below, tell us who wants to train, and we’ll help you choose the right program and location. You kept the flyer. You might as well see where this goes.”

### Alternate opening lines

Record all three while the camera is set:

- **A, recommended:** “Great. You didn’t throw the flyer away.”
- **B, drier:** “Well, the flyer made it off the windshield. That’s already progress.”
- **C, warmer:** “If you scanned this, something about Jiu-Jitsu is already on your mind.”

Do not say “you’re approved,” promise a transformation, quote unconfirmed pricing, or imply the viewer has committed to enrollment.

## 15-second cutdown

> “Great. You didn’t throw the flyer away. I’m Joao Crus, and I’ve taught Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu since 2003, from children age three to complete adult beginners. You don’t need to get in shape first. Tap below and we’ll help you find the right first class.”

Use this cut for a reminder ad or the top of a lightweight follow-up page, not as a substitute for the main welcome unless testing proves it converts better.

## Shot list

### Required footage

1. **Joao direct to camera, medium shot**
   - Quiet edge of the academy
   - Logo or recognizable mat space in frame
   - Eye-level camera, soft natural or diffused key light
   - Record the full script three times
2. **Joao greeting a beginner**
   - Handshake, friendly introduction, entering the mat
3. **Children's coaching detail**
   - Joao at child eye level
   - Purposeful movement or positional cue
   - A clearly visible tap-and-reset moment if permission allows
4. **Adult fundamentals coaching**
   - Joao correcting position, pressure, or timing
   - Avoid aggressive highlight-reel footage
5. **Room and community detail**
   - Students listening, drilling, resetting, smiling naturally
6. **Two clean CTA takes**
   - Joao leaves visual room below or beside him for the button and captions

### Capture settings

- Record 4K vertical if possible, 30 fps, shutter near 1/60.
- Use a lavalier or directional mic. Clean audio matters more than cinematic camera movement.
- Lock focus and exposure. Avoid bright windows behind Joao.
- Keep Joao centered enough to support 9:16, 4:5, and 16:9 crops.
- Record 10 seconds of room tone and 5–8 seconds of each B-roll action.
- Obtain permission for every identifiable student, especially minors.

## Edit notes

- Start on Joao. Do not open with a logo animation.
- Add burned-in captions in warm white with highlighted yellow keywords.
- Use the brand palette only: black, warm white, Champion Yellow, Academy Blue.
- Keep cuts energetic but human. No neon, glitch effects, fight-poster graphics, or generic stock footage.
- Music is optional and should remain low under speech.

### Current copy-lab structure

- **Archived original:** `/found-the-flyer-v2/` preserves the flyer-curiosity callback.
- **Control:** `/found-the-flyer-control/` is the approved pressure-response Iteration 1 and remains locked during the current iteration.
- **Active challenger:** `/found-the-flyer-active/` tests the next refinement of the approved belief.
- **Comparison workspace:** `/found-the-flyer-copy-lab/` shows both pages together, supports isolated views, section jumps, and optional synchronized scrolling.
- Never edit the control while a hypothesis is active. Record the hypothesis, active angle, primary metric, and decision before promoting or replacing either version.

### Approved hypothesis 01: pressure becomes practice

**Because** the control spends its strongest attention on the novelty of keeping and scanning the flyer, **we believe** reframing Jiu-Jitsu as a controlled place to practice how you respond under pressure will create more qualified first-class requests from parents and adults. **We will know this is directionally stronger** when reviewers consistently prefer its argument and, after launch-ready tracking exists, it improves valid first-class requests per unique QR session without reducing program/location completeness, booked classes, or attendance.

This is a message-angle comparison, not a statistically valid live A/B test yet. Do not call a winner from internal preference alone or from a small number of QR scans.

### Active hypothesis 02: life pushes back

**Because** the approved pressure-response angle is strategically stronger but still describes the benefit broadly, **we believe** leading with “Life pushes back. Practice what to do next.” and proving the coached resistance-reset loop will make the promise more concrete and increase qualified first-class requests. The active version must continue using a recommendation-based CTA and explicitly state that submitting the form does not automatically book or charge anything.

## Flyer production specification

### Review proof

- Source: `site/campaign/found-the-flyer-postcard.html`
- Print CSS: `site/assets/found-the-flyer-print.css`
- Intended trim: **9 × 6 inches**, landscape
- QR error correction: **H**
- QR quiet zone: 4 modules
- Destination is stored in `site/assets/found-the-flyer/qr-destination.txt`

### Printer handoff

The HTML proof shows content and layout direction. Before a commercial print run:

1. Ask the selected printer for its exact 9 × 6 template, bleed, safe area, color profile, and PDF standard.
2. Rebuild or export into that template with fonts embedded and images at effective print resolution.
3. Keep the QR at least 1.25 inches wide. The proof uses approximately 2.35 inches.
4. Print one physical proof at 100% and scan from several phones in daylight and low light.
5. Confirm the final domain route is live before printing. Never print a RawGitHack or Vercel preview URL.
6. Use a short visible fallback URL beneath the QR.

### Distribution safety

- Obtain explicit property-owner or manager permission before placing materials on vehicles in a parking lot.
- Do not place material where it blocks the driver’s view, interferes with the vehicle, or is likely to become litter.
- Follow local rules and venue policies. This campaign kit does not make a legal determination about vehicle distribution.
- Partner counters, school bags with permission, local events, and USPS EDDM are alternative distribution channels.
- USPS currently describes EDDM pieces as Marketing Mail flats and provides its own route-mapping tool. Recheck current dimensions, postage, and preparation requirements directly with USPS before converting this handout to EDDM.

## Tracking contract

### QR destination

```text
https://joaocrusbjj.com/found-the-flyer/?utm_source=car_flyer&utm_medium=offline&utm_campaign=found_the_flyer&utm_content=general_v1
```

Use a separate `utm_content` for every physical variation or placement batch:

| Variation | `utm_content` |
|---|---|
| General car flyer, version 1 | `general_v1` |
| Dripping Springs kids placement | `ds_kids_v1` |
| Austin / Castle Hill partner placement | `austin_partner_v1` |
| Local event handout | `event_[event_slug]_v1` |

Do not change the destination encoded in already printed cards. If destinations need flexibility, use a first-party redirect route that is owned and monitored, not a free public QR redirect service.

### Browser events

All events must remain consent-aware and exclude PII.

| Event | Trigger | Parameters |
|---|---|---|
| `campaign_landing_view` | Campaign JavaScript initializes | campaign name, medium, page |
| `video_start` | Visitor opens the video slot/player | video title, campaign name, page |
| `select_content` | Visitor selects child/adult/unsure path | content type, non-PII item ID, campaign |
| `lead_submit_attempt` | Existing shared form handler | form, lead type, program, location |
| `lead_submit_success` | Server confirms storage | same non-PII categories |
| `lead_submit_error` | Server rejects or fails | non-PII error type |

`generate_lead` should only be emitted after the server has confirmed the lead was stored. Name, email, phone, free text, and child details must not enter analytics.

### CRM fields and tags

- `lead_source`: `offline_flyer`
- `lead_campaign`: `found_the_flyer`
- `lead_program`: selected program
- `lead_location`: selected location
- preserve first/last-touch UTMs and supported click IDs through the existing attribution object
- HighLevel tag: `Campaign - Found the Flyer`
- pipeline stage: `New lead`
- immediate owner notification and staff follow-up task

### Success metrics

Track by printed batch, not only in aggregate:

1. QR sessions per 100 distributed pieces
2. video starts per QR session
3. lead submissions per QR session
4. qualified leads per 100 pieces
5. booked first classes
6. attended first classes
7. enrolled students
8. cost per attended class and cost per enrollment

Do not judge the campaign on QR scans alone.
