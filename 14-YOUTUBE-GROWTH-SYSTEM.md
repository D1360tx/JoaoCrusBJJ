> The model to borrow is not faceless AI content. It is an AI-assisted operating system that turns Joao's real expertise, teaching, stories, and class footage into consistent, measurable content with less manual coordination.

---

## 1. Strategic decision

### Apply

- Treat AI as a content operator, not a chatbot used for isolated tasks.
- Convert repeatable content work into a documented pipeline.
- Give every task one measurable outcome and an approval gate.
- Use tools for transcription, asset organization, script variants, editing instructions, publishing preparation, and analytics review.
- Build reusable templates so each recording does not restart from zero.

### Do not apply

- Do not launch a network of generic faceless channels.
- Do not replace Joao's face, voice, lineage, or academy footage with synthetic personalities.
- Do not optimize primarily for global vanity views.
- Do not permit an unattended loop to publish, make claims, spend ad budget, or contact leads.
- Do not flood the channel with low-value derivatives.

### Core principle

> **Joao is the authority and creative source. AI operates the production system around him.**

## 5. Repeatable content franchises

### A. Every Game Has a Job

**Positioning:**

> “A parent sees a game. An experienced coach sees what that game is teaching.”

**Format:** Real class footage followed by Joao explaining the activity's coaching purpose.

**Potential themes:**

- Coordination and body awareness
- Listening under movement
- Boundaries and safe contact
- Persistence after frustration
- Cooperative problem-solving
- Transitioning from play into technical learning

**Guardrail:** Describe observable teaching objectives. Avoid promising diagnosed developmental, behavioral, or medical outcomes.

### B. Joao Answers Parents

Short, clear answers to high-intent questions:

- Is age three too young for BJJ?
- What happens during the first class?
- What if my child is shy or distracted?
- How do you teach safety and tapping?
- Does my child need to be athletic?
- How should parents evaluate a children's BJJ program?

### C. Private Game Map

**Positioning:**

> “You may not need more techniques. You may need to know what matters in your positions and what to work on next.”

**Format:** Joao diagnoses a common game problem, demonstrates the decision sequence, and explains what a private assessment changes.

This is the best content franchise for the immediate private-coaching growth objective.

### D. Lineage in Practice

Show how Carlson Gracie, Ricardo De La Riva, and Joao's coaching history influence what happens on the mat today.

Each episode should connect a lineage story to a visible coaching principle. Lineage should function as a living influence, not merely a credential list.

### E. The Coach's Notebook

Reserve for a later phase after the local system is operating consistently. Use Joao's books, 101 games/drills, old DVD curriculum, and instructor testimonials to validate demand for coach education.

## 6. Production model

### Weekly source capture

Start from the existing preference for approximately one useful instructional anchor per week.

An anchor may be:

- A short Joao teaching session recorded specifically for content
- A real class demonstration
- A parent FAQ answer
- A private-session principle demonstrated with consent
- A podcast or book concept explained on camera
- A lineage story paired with mat footage

Whenever practical, capture clean horizontal and vertical footage during the same session. Use a stable camera, lavalier or close microphone, uncluttered background, and real academy branding.

### One anchor becomes a controlled asset package

The AI-assisted system prepares:

1. Source transcript
2. Claim and consent checklist
3. Long-form edit outline
4. Short/Reel cut recommendation
5. Hook and title options
6. Thumbnail concepts using real imagery
7. YouTube description, chapters, and CTA
8. Instagram/Facebook caption
9. Parent email or Beehiiv excerpt when relevant
10. Website FAQ or article excerpt when useful
11. Paid-ad concept only when the content naturally supports an approved offer

This is a production menu, not a requirement to publish every derivative.

## 7. Workflow

### Stage 1: Intake

Store the source video, transcript, date, participants, location, audience, offer, consent status, and desired business outcome.

### Stage 2: Extraction

AI identifies:

- Strongest truthful hook
- Distinct lesson or story
- Demonstration moments
- Questions raised by the audience
- Safe, supportable claims
- Appropriate CTA

### Stage 3: Packaging

Create long-form and short-form options with titles, thumbnails, descriptions, captions, editing notes, and destination URLs.

### Stage 4: Human review

Joao or Diego approves:

- Technical accuracy
- Tone and brand fit
- Minor/family consent
- Claims
- Price, schedule, and location details
- Final CTA

### Stage 5: Publish

Publish approved content to the canonical channel and selected secondary platforms. Preserve UTMs and destination-specific tracking.

### Stage 6: Measurement

Record performance after publication and compare it with the hook, topic, format, audience, and CTA.

### Stage 7: Learning loop

Use actual performance to recommend what to repeat, repackage, stop, or test next. AI may propose changes; humans retain publishing and budget authority.

## 9. Measurement framework

### Business metrics

- Qualified leads attributed to YouTube/content
- Class-match starts and completions
- Booked trial or deposit actions
- Private-coaching inquiries and bookings
- Email opt-ins by content series
- Assisted conversions where content precedes another channel

### YouTube diagnostics

- Impressions and thumbnail click-through rate
- Opening retention
- Average percentage viewed
- Returning viewers
- Saves, shares, and substantive questions
- Viewer geography, especially Austin/Dripping Springs relevance
- Description and pinned-comment clicks
- Subscriber conversion by series

### Interpretation rules

- High views with weak qualified traffic are awareness, not proven growth.
- Low impressions with strong retention may indicate packaging or distribution problems.
- Strong clicks with poor form completion indicate offer or landing-page friction.
- Parent questions are content-research inputs and should be tagged for future episodes.

- Which derivatives were useful versus unnecessary?
- Is there enough demand to continue weekly, expand, or separate an audience later?

## 11. Automation design

The implementation can use Hermes/OpenClaw or another agent stack. Ernesto's idea is vendor-independent: connect the operator to tools, assign measurable tasks, and build a repeatable system.

### Safe first automation

- File and metadata intake
- Speech-to-text transcription
- Clip candidate detection
- Script and title variants
- Description and chapter drafting
- Thumbnail briefs
- Content calendar preparation
- Analytics ingestion and weekly reporting
- Recommended next topics based on actual audience response

### Approval-required actions

- Publishing or scheduling
- Any use of minors' footage
- Paid promotion
- Price, guarantee, developmental, safety, or performance claims
- Replies presented as Joao
- Lead outreach
- Access to production credentials

### Technical safeguards

- Work from copies or sandboxed asset folders
- Limit API spending
- Preserve original media
- Log generated drafts and approvals
- Set runtime and batch-size limits
- Never point an autonomous experimental loop at the production website or canonical media library

## 12. Brand and compliance guardrails

- Use real Joao, coach, student, and academy imagery with appropriate consent.
- No generic AI children, fake coaches, fabricated testimonials, or synthetic lineage scenes presented as real.
- No guaranteed confidence, anti-bullying, developmental, belt, or competition outcomes.
- Do not publish unverified schedules, rates, uniform rules, locations, or offers.
- Protect minors' names and personal information.
- Use licensed music, footage, fonts, and third-party assets.
- Keep captions readable and mobile-first.
- Preserve Joao's natural voice rather than forcing viral slang or exaggerated hooks.
- Content should teach enough to establish authority while directing the right viewer toward the right offer, not function as an unstructured free curriculum archive.
