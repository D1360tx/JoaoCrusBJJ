# Gym Management Software Comparison: Zen Planner vs Wodify vs Kmura

**Prepared:** 2026-07-31  
**Decision context:** Joao Crus BJJ currently uses Zen Planner for billing and member management. Reported group headcount is 42, but the Zen Planner export must confirm the billable active-member count. Joao operates in Dripping Springs and teaches an Austin program inside Castle Hill Fitness.

## Executive decision

**Keep Zen Planner through the August 17 campaign launch. Do not migrate the billing system during a conversion launch.**

After launch, run structured demos of Wodify Accelerate and Kmura. Kmura is the most interesting challenger because it is BJJ-native, transparent, and unusually strong on curriculum and retention alerts. It is not yet a safe automatic replacement because several Joao-critical capabilities are not publicly documented.

### Current ranking for Joao

1. **Zen Planner: safest now.** Existing data, agreements, billing tokens, families, attendance, and history are already there.
2. **Kmura: best post-launch pilot candidate.** Strong BJJ workflows and transparent economics, subject to family, private-appointment, reporting, integration, security, and migration proof.
3. **Wodify: strongest mature growth platform on paper.** It appears capable but equivalent-plan pricing and processing economics are not public. It may be excessive for Joao's current scale.

A platform should win because it measurably improves leads, retention, private-lesson utilization, collections, or admin time. A newer interface or an “AI” label is not enough.

| 201–250 | $269 | $3,228 |

Add-ons/bundles:

- Website: $99/mo
- Engage CRM/marketing: $249/mo and includes a website
- Branded app: $39/mo
- EMV device: $39/mo
- Studio + website: $198–$388/mo
- Studio + website + Engage: $348–$538/mo

The reported 30 kids plus 12 adults equals 42 group students. If those are the only billable active members, Joao may fall in the $99 tier. The Zen export and invoice must confirm this before using the number.

### Wodify

Wodify currently advertises:

- Starting price reduced from $179 to $79/mo per location
- “$100/month off for life” when a demo is booked by July 31
- Essentials, Accelerate, and Ultimate plans

The site does **not** publish each tier's exact price. The advertised $79 is only a starting floor and cannot be treated as the price of Accelerate, which is the first plan containing family groups, belt tracking, lead management, at-risk identification, dashboards, custom reports, API access, and MCP access.

Annual floor scenarios:

| Locations | Promotional floor | Regular advertised floor |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $948 | $2,148 |
| 2 | $1,896 | $4,296 |

These are not equivalent-feature totals. A written Accelerate proposal is required.

### Kmura

- $150/mo per location
- Two months free under the current promotion
- All features and unlimited students
- No setup fee, no onboarding charge, no contract
- SMS is pay-as-you-go
- Card processing: 2.6% + $0.30

| Locations | First year with two free months | Ongoing annual |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $1,500 | $1,800 |
| 2 | $3,000 | $3,600 |

The critical contract question is whether Castle Hill counts as a second paid “location.” If yes, Kmura becomes $300/mo despite a modest combined student base.

### Payment-rate scenario

Kmura compares its 2.6% + $0.30 rate to an estimated Zen rate of approximately 2.99% + $0.45. **Zen does not publish that rate, so Kmura's comparison is not independently verified.**

If the comparison were accurate and Joao processed $100,000 annually by card, estimated savings would be:

| Annual transactions | Estimated annual savings |
|---:|---:|
| 500 | $465 |
| 720 | $498 |
| 1,000 | $540 |

At a confirmed $99 Zen base plan, one-location Kmura costs $612 more annually before processing. Under the 720-transaction scenario, claimed processing savings reduce that difference to roughly $114/year. That is essentially break-even, but only if all revenue is card volume and the Zen rate estimate is correct.

**Conclusion:** payment economics could justify Kmura at one location, but not from website claims alone. Use 12 months of real Zen statements and a written Finix proposal.

## 3. Detailed product comparison

### Member, family, and kids-program operations

**Zen Planner**

- Explicit family accounts
- Belt and skill tracking
- Shared curriculum
- Attendance and check-in
- Member self-service
- Member and staff apps
- Billing, memberships, retail, payroll, and reporting

**Wodify**

- Guardian-managed family groups and shared payments from Accelerate
- Belt progression based on attendance, skill mastery, time, or custom rules
- Certificates, streaks, milestones, app messaging, and content library
- Waitlists, announcements, retail, and upcoming-invoice purchases

**Kmura**

- Student profiles, attendance history, belt ranks, stripes, curriculum mastery, instructor notes, and classes-to-next-stripe
- Student portal with curriculum and attendance history
- Digital waivers through Documenso

**Joao implication:** Joao is kids-heavy. Guardian accounts, sibling billing, shared payment methods, minor waivers, and parent communications are hard requirements. Kmura cannot advance until it demonstrates them end-to-end.

### Private lessons and scheduling

- Wodify explicitly supports both classes and appointments.
- Zen provides online scheduling and membership/package management; Joao's exact private-package workflow should be tested.
- Kmura publicly documents weekly class schedules, instructor/location support, and check-in, but not appointment inventory, private packages, rescheduling, cancellation policies, or coach availability.

**Joao implication:** privates are a top revenue priority. Kmura must show a real workflow for the $3,800/40-class package, remaining-credit visibility, booking, cancellations, coach availability, and revenue recognition.

### Leads and marketing

**Zen Planner Studio** has marketing automations but advanced funnel visibility, two-way messaging, web chat, reputation tools, AI content, and 100+ campaigns sit in the $249 Engage product.

**Wodify Accelerate** includes a conversion board, nurture sequences, family management, dashboards, and 15 automations. Essentials includes inbox, texting, email/SMS templates, and two automations but not the lead conversion board.

**Kmura** includes a visual Kanban lead pipeline, bulk email/SMS, and AI alerts when leads go cold. The site does not publicly document form embedding, Meta Lead Ads ingestion, attribution fields, UTM persistence, review requests, web chat, or automation-builder depth.

**Joao implication:** the chosen platform must capture campaign source, offer, location, age group, booked class, attendance, enrollment, and collected revenue. A generic lead board is not enough.

### Retention and AI

Kmura's most differentiated feature is nightly monitoring for:

- Attendance-collapse and churn risk
- Leads needing follow-up
- Promotion readiness
- Schedule optimization
- Failed billing, expired waivers, and administrative issues

Wodify offers at-risk identification and business dashboards from Accelerate. Zen offers deep reporting and automation, but its public “AI” material is more focused on campaigns/content than BJJ retention.

Kmura's terms explicitly state that AI is probabilistic and may be inaccurate. Therefore, the alerts should be treated as prioritization suggestions, not automated decisions.

**Demo proof required:** use anonymized real records and ask Kmura to show what triggered an alert, whether thresholds are editable, false-positive controls, who can see minor data, and whether messages always require human approval.

### Apps, portals, and daily usability

- Wodify offers a unified app for clients and coaches and sells a branded-app add-on.
- Zen offers member and staff apps plus a $39 branded-app option.
- Kmura shows student and instructor web portals. It does not publicly promise App Store/Google Play native apps, push notifications, offline check-in, or device management.

A polished website demo does not prove front-desk reliability. Joao should personally complete check-in, payment update, family billing, private booking, belt promotion, refund, and failed-payment recovery on a phone before considering migration.

### Reporting, API, and integrations

**Zen Planner** publicly lists Perkville, Wellhub, Welld Health, HybridAF, ClassPass, SugarWOD, Facebook, Yext, Mailchimp, Optum, Google Analytics, Slack, Zapier, Google Calendar, Microsoft 365, Google Drive, YouTube, TikTok, WhatsApp, Shopify, Printful, and a custom API. Many marketing connections are Engage-dependent.

**Wodify** lists QuickBooks, Facebook Leads, Slack, ClassPass, API access, and MCP access. API/MCP begin at Accelerate.

**Kmura** names Finix, Twilio, SendGrid, Documenso, and AWS/Replit in its terms/privacy documents. No public accounting integration, Zapier connector, public API, webhook catalog, Meta Leads integration, GA4 integration, or broad partner directory was found.

**Joao implication:** Kmura should not replace Zen until it demonstrates clean exports or APIs for attribution, finance, custom landing pages, and agency reporting.

## 4. Platform maturity and risk

### Zen Planner

Strengths:

- Existing operating history inside Joao's business
- Rich martial-arts workflows
- Broad reports, apps, family support, and integrations
- Phone, chat, email, and help-center support
- Free onboarding assistance when migrating into Zen

Weaknesses:

- Interface and product packaging can feel complex
- Advanced CRM requires a costly Engage extension
- Pricing increases with active-member count
- Processing price is not publicly transparent
- Its FAQ currently contradicts itself by saying month-to-month and also “12-month commitment”; contract terms must come from Joao's agreement

### Wodify

Strengths:

- Broad operational, retention, CRM, reporting, and app capabilities
- Unlimited clients/employees
- BJJ-specific family, rank, curriculum, and event features
- Public product roadmap, API documentation, training, and migration support
- Vendor claims 5,000+ businesses

Weaknesses:

- Higher-tier and processing prices are hidden behind sales
- Important BJJ features start at Accelerate
- Add-ons can undermine the all-in-one message
- Per-location pricing may penalize Joao's Castle Hill satellite program
- Its own comparison page overstates some differences with Zen; vendor comparison claims should not be accepted as neutral evidence

### Kmura

Strengths:

- Best BJJ-native product framing
- Transparent software and card-processing prices
- Curriculum, promotion, attendance, instructor notes, and Portuguese support
- AI alerts tied to actual academy operations rather than generic content generation
- Free full-history onboarding claim and data-export rights in terms
- No contract and 90-day post-termination export window

Risks and open issues:

- Newer/smaller public footprint; only a small set of public ambassador testimonials was found
- No public uptime history, SLA, status page, SOC 2 report, penetration-test summary, DPA, or MFA claim found
- Privacy policy describes encryption and tokenization, but security detail is high-level
- No public API/integration catalog found
- Family billing and private-appointment workflows are not documented
- Native mobile apps are not documented
- Custom reporting depth, audit logs, role permissions, dunning, chargeback tools, and accounting integration are not documented
- Terms rely on Finix, Twilio/SendGrid, Documenso, and cloud hosts and heavily limit Kmura's liability for third-party failures
- The gym is responsible for consent, COPPA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, waiver legality, refunds, disputes, and chargebacks
- Kmura may use anonymized/aggregated data to improve AI models; this data license survives termination
- Data exports are supplied “within a reasonable timeframe,” not under a defined SLA

None of these automatically disqualifies Kmura. They make a controlled pilot and written due diligence mandatory.

## 5. Joao-specific recommendation

### Do now

1. Keep Zen Planner as the billing and member source of truth through the August 17 launch.
2. Obtain the current Zen invoice, contract, payment statements, active-member count, failed-payment report, and feature usage.
3. Confirm whether Dripping Springs and Castle Hill are one or two billable locations under each vendor.
4. Book structured demos of **Wodify Accelerate** and **Kmura** using the same script below.
5. Do not upload production data during a demo.

### Preferred evaluation order

**First: Kmura sandbox.** It offers the clearest chance of a meaningful operational improvement at Joao's scale.

**Second: Wodify Accelerate quote.** Evaluate only after receiving exact plan, processing, SMS, onboarding, and two-location prices.

**Third: Zen optimization.** Before switching, determine whether Joao is simply underusing existing family, curriculum, reporting, automation, scheduling, and payment-recovery features.

### Switch only if the challenger proves all gates

- [ ] Family/guardian and sibling billing works cleanly
- [ ] Minor waivers and consent records are complete
- [ ] Private 40-class packages and appointment booking work
- [ ] Existing agreements, attendance, ranks, payment tokens, waivers, notes, and balances migrate
- [ ] Parallel billing test reconciles to the penny
- [ ] Dunning, refunds, disputes, and chargebacks are operational
- [ ] Austin location treatment is acceptable
- [ ] GA4/Meta/source attribution survives form-to-enrollment flow
- [ ] Data export, deletion, ownership, DPA, security, and breach procedures are written
- [ ] Support response commitments are written
- [ ] Staff and parent usability tests pass
- [ ] A rollback plan exists and Zen remains active until reconciliation passes

## 6. Demo script

Ask each vendor to perform these actions live, not merely say “yes”:

1. Create one family with two children, one payer, different memberships, and separate waivers.
2. Sell a 12-month agreement with a 60-day termination notice.
3. Sell a $3,800 private package with 40 credits and book/reschedule/cancel a lesson.
4. Import a Meta lead with source, campaign, ad, offer, location, age group, and consent timestamp.
5. Trigger email/SMS follow-up, opt out, and display the consent log.
6. Check a child into class and update belt/stripe/curriculum progress.
7. Process failed payment, card update, refund, and chargeback.
8. Show Dripping Springs and Castle Hill schedules with one member attending both.
9. Show churn/attendance alerts and explain the rule behind each alert.
10. Export all member, billing, attendance, rank, waiver, lead, and attribution data.
11. Show roles, audit history, MFA, backup/recovery, uptime, and incident response.
12. Provide total written pricing for one-location and two-location configurations.

## 7. Bottom line

### Best immediate choice

**Zen Planner**, because migration risk is greater than the current evidence of benefit.

### Most promising challenger

**Kmura**, if Joao's operation is treated as one billable location and the demo proves family management, private appointments, integrations, and security. Its one-location economics may be nearly cost-neutral after processing savings, but that depends on real statements.

### Most capable broad alternative

**Wodify Accelerate**, but no buying decision should be made from the $79 headline. The equivalent BJJ plan and payment rates remain undisclosed.

**Final recommendation:** optimize Zen now, pilot Kmura after the launch, and use Wodify as the mature benchmark. Replace Zen only after a parallel, reversible migration proves measurable improvement.
