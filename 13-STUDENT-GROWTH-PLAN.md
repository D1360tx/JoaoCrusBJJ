## 1. DIAGNOSIS — why "90 leads/month" agencies keep failing

The failed agencies all run the same play: FB ads → lead form → free class. Doc 12's verified sweep shows **every local school runs the identical free-trial offer** — so the ad is a commodity, the lead form takes 4 seconds and commits nobody, and "free" filters for tire-kickers (hence the documented no-show problem, 08 §3.9). Pay-on-signup sounds great but just means the agency abandons the account when cheap leads don't convert.

**Conclusion: this is an offer + intent problem, not a lead-volume problem.** The fix is a better offer with built-in commitment, not more of the same leads. (Joao's own read — "the offer isn't good enough" — matches the doc-12 evidence.)

## 4. DIRECT MAIL — the uncontested channel

No DS school shows any sign of mail marketing (all FB-or-nothing). This is a measurable test, not a spend commitment — framed in Joao's "data doesn't lie" language.

### Mechanics
- **Vehicle: USPS EDDM** (Every Door Direct Mail) — pick carrier routes by geography/demographics, no mailing list needed. Ballpark **~$0.35–0.40/household all-in** (≈$0.24 postage + $0.10–0.15 print). ⚠️ *Verify current EDDM retail rates + print quotes before giving Joao numbers.*
- **Budget frame:** ~$2,000 test ≈ 2.5 months of his current ~$25/day FB spend.

### The 3-touch cadence (depth beats breadth)
**Same households, 3 drops, ~2–3 weeks apart.** One cold postcard converts <1%; response climbs on repeat drops because you catch families at different decision moments (rough week at school, bullying incident, season change). At a fixed budget:
- 5,700 homes × 1 touch → seen once, forgotten
- **~1,900 homes × 3 touches → "the age-3 BJJ school" becomes a known thing in a defined area** ✅
If cost-per-lead after drop 3 is good → scale routes/touches with data. "Everyday mail" = same instinct, no cap, no measurement window.
