- **Headline:** Tap Means Stop
- **Description:** Youth BJJ, Tue/Thu at 5:00 p.m.
- **Creative:** Real Youth partner-practice still. Crop tightly enough to show both students and a visible coach. On-image copy: `TAP MEANS STOP.` Secondary line: `AGES 8–12 · CENTRAL AUSTIN`.

### AY02_TAP-RESET-TRY-AGAIN_VIDEO

- **Primary text:** Tap. Stop. Reset. Try again. Youth students need a clear way to communicate during partner practice and a partner who responds. See how Joao teaches boundaries and body control in the Austin ages 8–12 class.

## Current-campaign evidence note

The Dripping Springs campaign delivered most spend to its age-3 ad. From Aug. 27 through Sep. 5, that ad received 93.76% of spend and 13 of 14 Meta-attributed leads. The Program Fit ad recorded one lead on limited spend. Tap Means Stop and Confidence had high early link CTR but extremely small delivery. These figures do not establish transferable winners for Austin. The Austin test should keep each audience and hook measurable without assuming the age-3 result applies to Youth or Adults.
