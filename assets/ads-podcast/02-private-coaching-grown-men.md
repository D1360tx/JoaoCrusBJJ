## 1. Original source

Diego trained privately with Chris and learned why Joao's private instruction fits him:

### Source guardrails

- Keep Chris anonymous in public materials unless he gives explicit permission for his name, age, occupation, story, image, and voice.
- Confirm whether “judge from Dallas at age 72” is the correct interpretation of Diego's voice note. Verify the person's age, title, rank, relationship to Joao, and permission before publishing any identifying details.
- Ask Joao to define “old man jiu-jitsu” in his own words before using it as a teaching claim.
- Do not claim Carlson Gracie jiu-jitsu is categorically “more technical” than other schools. All legitimate BJJ is technical. The differentiated story is how Joao applies pressure, positional control, timing, efficiency, and lineage to the individual student.

## 2. Core insight

> Serious adults do not lose interest in jiu-jitsu. Their calendar, body, and tolerance for random training stop matching a one-size-fits-all class.

The strongest market is not merely “older successful men.” It is **experienced or jiu-jitsu-curious adults with demanding lives who want an intelligent, individualized way to keep progressing.** Age, executive responsibility, prior training, and physical wear make the message more specific, but the public promise should remain aspirational rather than ageist.

## 3. Audience and job to be done

### Primary audience

- Men roughly 45–65 in Dripping Springs and Austin
- Owners, executives, senior professionals, judges, attorneys, physicians, operators, and consultants
- Former practitioners who miss the sport but no longer fit a conventional class schedule
- Current practitioners who feel stuck, train inconsistently, or want to build a game around their body
- Beginners attracted to the intellectual depth of jiu-jitsu but wary of competition-gym intensity

### What they are buying

- Scheduling that fits a demanding week
- A private learning environment without the need to keep up with a room of younger athletes
- A game plan built around their body, experience, injuries, and goals
- Faster understanding through immediate feedback
- Access to Joao's interpretation of Carlson Gracie pressure and positional control
- A durable path to continue training as they get older

### Emotional tension

They do not want a watered-down version of jiu-jitsu. They want **serious jiu-jitsu taught intelligently enough to remain part of their life.**

## 5. Creative angles

### Angle A: Your calendar is not a character flaw

**Hook:** “You still love jiu-jitsu. Your calendar just stopped cooperating.”

**Development:** Fixed class schedules are difficult for executives, business owners, parents, and professionals. Private coaching offers a focused appointment and a plan for the time they actually have.

**Close:** “Build a jiu-jitsu practice that fits the life you built.”

### Angle B: Stop training like you are 25

**Hook:** “You do not need to train like a 25-year-old to get better at jiu-jitsu.”

**Development:** The goal is not easier jiu-jitsu. It is more intelligent jiu-jitsu: better position, pressure, timing, frames, and decisions instead of depending on pace or athleticism.

**Close:** “Serious training. Built around the body you have now.”

### Angle C: Old man jiu-jitsu

**Hook:** “Old man jiu-jitsu is not slow jiu-jitsu. It is jiu-jitsu that wastes less.”

**Development:** Ask Joao to demonstrate one sequence where position and pressure remove the need for speed. The phrase should be explained immediately so it feels earned and self-aware, not insulting.

**Close:** “Less scrambling. More control.”

**Test note:** Use “old man jiu-jitsu” in selected ads and videos, not automatically as the premium offer name. It may create strong recognition among practitioners while narrowing or cheapening the main landing-page position.

### Angle D: Pressure is a system

**Hook:** “Pressure is not about being heavier. It is about giving the other person fewer good decisions.”

**Development:** Show Joao teaching angle, connection, weight placement, and positional control. This translates Carlson Gracie lineage into something visible rather than using lineage as a credential alone.

**Close:** “Learn pressure you can explain, repeat, and make your own.”

### Angle E: The art can grow old with you

**Hook:** “What if your best jiu-jitsu is still ahead of you?”

**Development:** If verified and permissioned, the 72-year-old Dallas judge story becomes proof of a long horizon. The story is about sustained learning and adaptation, not a promise that every student will earn a black belt.

**Close:** “Build a game for the next decade, not just the next round.”

### Angle F: Return to the mat

**Hook:** “You did not quit loving jiu-jitsu. Life interrupted the schedule.”

**Development:** Speak to former students who fear being out of shape, behind, injured, or embarrassed. A private session reduces the social and physical friction of returning.

**Close:** “Start where you are. Build from there.”

### Angle G: Private access to a living lineage

**Hook:** “Carlson Gracie's influence should be something you can feel in the position, not just read in a bio.”

**Development:** Joao explains one principle Carlson emphasized and then applies it to the student's game. This supports Diego's broader direction to present lineage as a living influence.

**Close:** “Learn the principle. Feel the difference. Make it part of your game.”

## 6. Offer concepts for approval

These are positioning concepts only. Do not publish format, duration, frequency, price, guarantee, or availability until Joao approves them.

### Private Game Map

A diagnostic first session that identifies the student's current strengths, recurring positional problems, physical constraints, and the first training priorities.

### Executive Private Training

Recurring one-on-one instruction positioned around flexible appointments, focused progress, discretion, and efficient use of time. “Executive” should be tested against a broader “Private Coaching” version to avoid sounding exclusionary.

### Pressure Jiu-Jitsu Intensive

A finite instructional block focused on pressure, positional control, and a small connected system. Best for experienced practitioners and lineage-driven creative.

### Return to the Mat Session

A lower-friction re-entry offer for former practitioners who need assessment, confidence, and a plan before joining open group training.

### Jiu-Jitsu for the Long Game

A coaching plan framed around durable technique and continued practice as the student gets older. This is likely the strongest umbrella promise for a premium landing page.
