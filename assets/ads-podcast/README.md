# Ads and Podcast Copy Workspace

This directory is the working home for Joao Crus BJJ advertising concepts, podcast stories, video scripts, hooks, landing-page message match, and creative iteration notes.

## Purpose

The landing pages establish the campaign promises. This workspace turns those promises into stories and media that can be recorded, tested, measured, and improved without losing the connection between the ad and the page.

## Working rules

1. Start with a real parent, student, or coach moment.
2. Connect the moment to one clear lesson or transformation.
3. Match each ad to the page where the viewer lands.
4. Use real academy footage and real conversations whenever possible.
5. Explain BJJ language before using terms such as rolling or tapping.
6. Keep competitive claims specific and supportable.
7. Frame personal beliefs as personal beliefs, not universal guarantees.
8. Do not use em dashes in Joao website, ad, or podcast copy.
9. Do not publish prices, trial terms, guarantees, or hard offer details until approved.
10. Record what changed between iterations and why.

## Status labels

- **Source:** Raw story, transcript, or observation
- **Working:** Structured concept being developed
- **Ready to record:** Script and shot plan approved
- **Recorded:** Source footage exists
- **Ready to test:** Final edit, copy, destination, and tracking are complete
- **Testing:** Live with a defined variable and measurement window
- **Winner:** Supported by performance data
- **Retired:** Preserved with the reason it was stopped

## Iteration workflow

1. Preserve the original source story.
2. Identify the emotional hook, mechanism, and outcome.
3. Draft distinct creative angles rather than minor wording variations.
4. Create long, medium, and short versions.
5. Confirm message match with the destination page.
6. Confirm permissions for personal stories, names, and footage.
7. Record once, then cut multiple ads and organic clips from the same conversation.
8. Track hook, hold rate, click-through rate, landing-page conversion, and qualified-lead rate.
9. Add results and the next hypothesis to the concept file.
