## Executive read

This funnel uses a rank as both the targeting device and product architecture.

The creative does almost no explaining. Roger's face, the verified account, the physical purple belt, and the words **“Purple Belt Essentials”** identify:

- Who the product is for
- What stage it serves
- Why Roger has authority
- What the viewer should tap to discover

The landing page then expands the simple visual promise into a detailed advanced-skills curriculum and offers a low-friction $49.99 lifetime purchase.

## Creative anatomy

### Visual hook

Roger holds the purple belt vertically through the frame. The belt is not decoration. It is the product's strongest targeting and aspiration object.

A viewer can understand the stage before reading any copy.

### Copy

> **PURPLE BELT ESSENTIALS**
>
> **ROGER'S LATEST COURSE**
>
> **Tap to unlock offer**

The creative omits price, lesson count, testimonials, and curriculum details. “Unlock offer” turns the destination into a curiosity reveal.

### Authority

- Roger appears prominently
- Account is verified
- Roger Gracie TV branding is clear
- No additional credential stack is needed in the Story

### Weakness

A cold viewer who does not already know Roger gets little teaching proof or product explanation. This is efficient for a famous authority but difficult for a local instructor to copy directly.

## Landing-page mechanism

The course describes purple-belt development as a shift:

> From a solid white/blue-belt foundation into a more creative, personalized attacking framework.

This is stronger than merely selling “advanced techniques.” It gives the buyer an identity transition:

1. Foundation already built
2. Advanced stage begins
3. Options branch outward
4. Student develops a personal stamp

The extensive curriculum then proves that the course covers enough positions to support that transition.

## Offer architecture

### One-time anchor

- $49.99
- Lifetime access
- Stage-specific program
- No subscription required

### Subscription upsell

- $21.99 monthly for 1,000+ lessons
- $189.99 annually for 1,000+ lessons

The page uses the course as a self-liquidating front-end or subscription entry point. Buyers can choose certainty and ownership of one program or breadth across the whole library.

## Ad-to-page message match

### Strong

- Exact purple-belt stage
- Same Roger visual and branding
- “Latest course” leads to a dedicated product
- “Unlock offer” reveals a concrete one-time price
- Story's physical belt carries into checkout product art
- UTMs persist into the exact one-time checkout

### Friction

- The creative promises an “offer” without previewing its price or value
- Landing page introduces three price paths after a singular CTA
- “Your path to purple belt” may imply promotion progress more strongly than the product can guarantee
- Quantity changes from 62 classes to 63 videos
- Checkout strips away most proof and reassurance

## Why this funnel is useful to study

1. **Stage-specific segmentation:** One rank creates a clear audience and product boundary.
2. **Visual shorthand:** The belt communicates the audience before the headline is read.
3. **Identity transition:** The product is framed as the shift into a personalized advanced game.
4. **Curriculum as proof:** Specific techniques make an intangible course concrete.
5. **Authority compression:** Roger's image and name replace paragraphs of biography.
6. **Simple front-end price:** $49.99 lifetime access avoids subscription resistance.
7. **Ascension path:** Full-library monthly and annual subscriptions are available without removing the one-time option.
8. **Checkout continuity:** The exact product, image, price, and UTMs remain visible.

## Direct Joao applications

### 1. Use the belt as segmentation, not a promotion promise

Joao can create stage-specific content or assessment paths without claiming that a course or private package awards rank.

Examples:

- **White Belt Foundations Assessment**
- **Blue Belt Game-Building Session**
- **Competition Readiness Review**
- **Parent and Child First-Stage Roadmap**

Each should diagnose an actual training need, not sell rank advancement.

### 2. Show the object that represents the audience

Roger uses a purple belt. Joao's local offers can use real, instantly legible objects and moments:

- A child's first belt tied by Joao
- A parent watching the first class
- A student's competition notebook or game map
- Joao physically mapping positions during a private lesson

The object must support a truthful offer rather than manufacture emotion.

### 3. Package private coaching around identity transitions

A private assessment can be framed as moving from:

- Collecting techniques to understanding a personal game
- Surviving positions to making intentional decisions
- General classes to a prioritized training roadmap

This combines the strongest ideas from Roger, HPU, and Matt Arroyo without promising belt speed.

### 4. Build an ascension path

A possible future ladder, pending offer and price approval:

1. Focused diagnostic or assessment
2. Private lesson package
3. Ongoing private coaching
4. Digital support materials
5. Coach/curriculum offer for instructors

Do not publish this ladder until Joao confirms mechanics, rates, booking, and digital-product ownership.

## Weaknesses to avoid

- Using “path to [belt]” in a way that implies guaranteed promotion
- Hiding all practical value behind curiosity
- Showing inconsistent lesson counts
- Sending buyers from one promised offer into excessive choice
- Removing proof and reassurance at checkout
- Using rank symbolism without demonstrating Joao's actual coaching process
