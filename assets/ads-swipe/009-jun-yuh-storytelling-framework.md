## 1. What the creative does

### Hook

> “Storytelling psychology is the reason why I've made over $10 million without ever having to niche down.”

The opening combines a large self-reported outcome with a contrarian premise. It creates curiosity around the mechanism rather than opening with the webinar.

### Framework

Jun says people trust a whole human, not a fragment. He organizes a person into four dimensions, then shows how one belief can generate stories across each dimension. The useful mechanism is not the exact wheel. It is the shift from **topic-first expertise** to **belief-led stories expressed through different parts of a real life**.

### Demonstration

The Reel does not stop at “tell stories.” It demonstrates the method using one belief about freedom:

- being present with a partner and children;
- watching parents trade their lives for money;
- pursuing physical, mental, and spiritual health;
- sharing a personal-finance journey.

That turns an abstract framework into visible applications.

### Contrast

The enemy is fragmented, replaceable information:

- only teach;
- only sell;
- only prove expertise.

The conclusion is that information becomes easier to replace in the age of AI, while a person who earns trust and connection is harder to replace.

### Visual execution

- One informal talking-head setup for the full Reel
- Large white burned-in captions with only a few words visible at once
- Collage and handwritten-card overlays as proof objects
- Simple equation graphics such as “topic ≠ story”
- Red X marks to summarize the rejected behavior
- No elaborate B-roll, which keeps production repeatable
- No visible final CTA card; the spoken close ends on “easy,” and the caption carries the offer

## 2. What the landing page does

### Narrative sequence

1. **Outcome and immediate registration:** loyal audience in 72 hours, free ticket, short form.
2. **Founder origin story:** broke medical student to first family millionaire through storytelling.
3. **Audience objections:** not interesting, camera fear, judgment, uncertainty, and lack of ideas.
4. **New belief:** the visitor already has stories and needs a system.
5. **Three-day mechanism:** diagnose weak content, become the niche, turn stories into ideas.
6. **Format proof:** examples of story-led posts and creator results.
7. **Business bridge:** storytelling creates trust, and trust can create customers.
8. **Deliverables:** six concrete takeaways.
9. **Repeated registration:** forms appear at the hero, middle, and end.
10. **Social proof and legal footer:** participant proof, privacy, terms, and earnings disclaimer.

### Strong patterns

- The founder's origin story appears before curriculum details.
- Objections are written as the visitor's private thoughts.
- The page repeatedly alternates story, mechanism, proof, and CTA.
- The product is framed as a shared live experience, not merely lessons.
- Visual examples make the content formats tangible.

### Weaknesses and risks

- The Reel's public creative has no visible offer or destination in its final frame. It depends on the caption or a paid CTA layer.
- The page's 72-hour promise is attention-grabbing but compresses a three-day event into stronger outcome language.
- Creator claims around revenue, followers, and results are self-reported.
- The page says EST for an August event, while the Instagram caption says EDT.
- The forms show only name and email; consent and attribution behavior were not submitted or verified.
- Some copy repeats the same trust thesis instead of adding new proof.

## 3. Durable lesson for Joao

The main lesson is not “tell more stories.” It is:

> **Make the real human moment carry the teaching mechanism, then let the offer become the next chapter.**

Joao already has stronger raw material than most local academies:

- direct Carlson Gracie and De La Riva lineage;
- founding the academy in 2003;
- developing a Montessori-informed teaching practice;
- teaching children from age three;
- Diego and Kaiden's story about tapping, control, and earned confidence;
- Chris, a 52-year-old executive who needs serious jiu-jitsu to fit a demanding schedule;
- the reported older Dallas judge Joao promoted to black belt, pending verification and permission;
- long-term students, families, coaches, and returning adults.

The opportunity is to stop presenting those as separate credentials and convert them into **scene → tension → coaching belief → visible method → invitation**.

## 4. Joao adaptation framework

Do not copy Jun's four-part wheel. Use a Joao-specific story bank:

| Story lens | Question | Joao examples |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | What made Joao teach this way? | Carlson/De La Riva influence, academy founded in 2003, Montessori-informed evolution |
| Belief | What does Joao believe that changes the lesson? | Control before aggression, pressure without wasted movement, every game has a job |
| Student moment | When did the belief become real for someone? | Kaiden learning to tap, Chris adapting training to executive life, a toddler following one instruction |
| Teaching proof | What can viewers see Joao doing differently? | Position correction, purposeful game, parent explanation, individualized private game map |

### Ad spine

1. Open on a specific human moment.
2. Name the tension the viewer recognizes.
3. Reveal Joao's belief.
4. Show the coaching mechanism in action.
5. Translate it into the viewer's desired future.
6. Invite one clear next step.

### Landing-page spine

1. Audience-specific promise in the hero.
2. One real story that makes the problem emotionally legible.
3. Joao's belief and why he teaches this way.
4. Observable program or coaching mechanism.
5. Relevant proof from the same audience.
6. Process and objections.
7. Repeated CTA with approved terms.

## 5. Page-by-page incorporation

### Private coaching

**Lead story:** Chris loves jiu-jitsu, but an executive calendar makes conventional training inconsistent.

**Mechanism:** private instruction fits the person's schedule and builds a game around pressure, position, timing, body, and experience.

**Supporting story:** the older Dallas judge, only after the age, rank, wording, and permission are verified.

**CTA:** request a private coaching conversation or approved diagnostic session.

### Little Champions / toddler

**Lead story:** a three-year-old does not need a lecture or random play. Joao learned to turn short games into specific practice opportunities.

**Mechanism:** every game has a job.

**Proof:** real class footage showing cue, action, reset, and parent-visible behavior.

### Kids and Youth

**Lead story:** Diego/Kaiden or another permissioned family moment where tapping, listening, boundaries, or persistence becomes emotionally meaningful.

**Mechanism:** control creates safety, and safety allows earned confidence.

### Adults

**Lead story:** a returning practitioner who did not stop loving jiu-jitsu; life interrupted the schedule.

**Mechanism:** beginner-safe or returner-safe progression without competition-gym posturing.

### About / lineage

**Lead story:** one specific Carlson or De La Riva lesson Joao still teaches today.

**Mechanism:** demonstrate the principle in class so lineage becomes a living influence, not a name list.

## 6. Initial story-led creative concepts

### Private: The calendar did not mean he quit

> Chris still loved jiu-jitsu. What stopped fitting was the schedule.
>
> At 52, with an executive role and a full week, he did not need less serious training. He needed serious training built around the time and body he actually had.
>
> That is the purpose of private coaching with Joao Crus: pressure, position, timing, and a game designed for the person in front of him.

Public use requires Chris's permission or anonymization.

### Toddler: The first tap

> A tap is a small movement with a big meaning. It says, “I need you to stop,” and it teaches the other child to listen.
>
> That is why Joao does not treat toddler jiu-jitsu as random games. Every activity has a job, including body control, safe boundaries, and listening to a partner.

Use Diego's approved Kaiden story and real class proof rather than generic claims.

### Lineage: Pressure you can see

> Joao does not want Carlson Gracie's name to sit in a bio. He wants students to feel the lesson in the position.
>
> Show one correction in angle, connection, or weight placement, then explain why it creates control without a frantic scramble.

Joao must confirm the exact principle and recollection before publication.

## 7. Implementation rules

- Use real Joao/student/parent moments, not fabricated composite testimonials.
- Obtain permission before using names, ages, occupations, images, voices, rank histories, or identifiable personal details.
- Keep one story tied to one audience and one offer.
- Show the teaching mechanism. Do not rely on sentimental narration alone.
- Use real academy footage as proof whenever possible.
- Avoid turning every page into a long founder biography. Story earns attention; program clarity and CTA still close the lead.
- Keep public copy free of em dashes.
- Do not publish unconfirmed offers, prices, trial terms, schedules, injury claims, belt outcomes, or universal transformations.
- For Meta and Google, optimize toward qualified leads, booked/attended classes, enrollment, and purchase once HighLevel feedback is verified.

## 8. Test plan

| Landing-page order | Story before mechanism | Mechanism before story | Story-first improves engagement; mechanism-first may convert high-intent Search visitors faster. |

## 9. Recommendation

Adopt story-led creative as a system, not a one-off style:

- record one real Joao, parent, or student conversation each week;
- extract one human moment and one teaching belief;
- produce one 45–75 second story-led video, one 15–25 second paid cut, one email, and one matching page module;
- publish only the derivatives that fit current capacity and permissions;
- evaluate based on qualified academy actions, not views alone.

The strongest immediate pilot is **Chris's schedule conflict → serious private coaching built for the long game**, because private sessions are already a top business priority and the source story naturally connects audience, tension, mechanism, authority, and offer.
