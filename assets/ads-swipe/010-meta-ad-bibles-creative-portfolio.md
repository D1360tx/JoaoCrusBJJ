## What the portfolios reveal

These advertisers are not searching for one perfect ad. They keep the offer, product mechanism, and destination comparatively stable while rotating:

- audience hook;
- presenter and creator;
- opening visual;
- video duration;
- UGC, demonstration, static, advertorial, and VSL formats;
- emotional, educational, testimonial, and offer-led angles;
- proof order and risk reversal.

The reusable architecture is:

**audience moment → problem → distinctive mechanism → proof → offer → risk reversal → CTA**

The highest-value lesson is portfolio design, not copying any individual claim, song, script, or visual.

## Framework for Joao and local businesses

### Local-budget launch wave

For an approximately $35/day local campaign, begin with **6–9 creatives**, not 50 or 1,200 active ads.

1. Choose one audience, one offer, and one matched landing page.
2. Build three meaningful hook families:
   - recognizable pain or human moment;
   - contrarian belief or category reframe;
   - desired outcome or identity.
3. Produce two formats for each winning concept:
   - 15–25-second paid-social cut;
   - 45–75-second story or mechanism cut.
4. Add two retargeting concepts:
   - real proof or testimonial;
   - objection, FAQ, process, schedule, or what-happens-next creative.
5. Test an optional 2–4-minute VSL only after short-form signals justify a deeper explanation.
6. Keep each ad matched to the audience-specific page and CTA.
7. Optimize on qualified leads, booked visits, attendance, enrollment, and private bookings. Use watch rate and CTR as diagnostics.

### Testing hierarchy

1. Concept or angle
2. Hook
3. Presenter or visual format
4. Proof order
5. CTA
6. Minor copy and design changes

Do not fragment a small local budget across dozens of near-identical ads. Promote meaningful winners, pause weak concepts, and add deliberate variants.

## Joao concept bank

### Little Champions, ages 3–7

- **Hook:** “A 3-year-old doesn’t need long lines and lectures. Every game needs a job.”
- **Mechanism:** short, purposeful activities that practice listening, body control, safe boundaries, taking turns, and trying again.
- **Proof:** real Joao-led class footage showing distinct cues and activities.
- **CTA:** “See how the age 3–7 class works.”
- **Guardrail:** do not promise developmental outcomes or imply controlled BJJ-at-age-3 evidence.

### Safety and boundaries

- **Hook:** “The first skill isn’t fighting. It’s knowing when to stop.”
- **Mechanism:** control creates safety; safety makes earned confidence possible.
- **Proof:** a permission-cleared tapping or boundary moment plus Joao’s explanation.
- **CTA:** audience-matched class inquiry.
- **Guardrail:** do not claim tapping eliminates injury or guarantees behavior outside class.

### Private coaching

- **Hook:** “You didn’t quit jiu-jitsu. Your schedule stopped fitting group training.”
- **Mechanism:** appointments, direct correction, and a game adapted to pressure, position, timing, body, experience, and schedule.
- **Proof:** Joao correcting one position and explaining the purpose.
- **CTA:** “Request a private coaching assessment.”

### Adult returners

- **Hook:** “Starting again doesn’t mean starting from zero.”
- **Mechanism:** intelligent pacing, partner matching, beginner-safe or returner-safe instruction, and visible Joao coaching.
- **CTA:** confirmed Dripping Springs class or Austin private-instruction path, depending on location.
- **Guardrail:** do not invent an Austin recurring adult group schedule.

### Proof and objection retargeting

Use real permission-cleared parent or student proof, visible instruction, current schedule/location, and a clear explanation of the next step after form submission. Keep unconfirmed trial prices, membership prices, bonuses, deposits, guarantees, and capacity claims out of creatives.

## Local-business translation

| Business type | Creative structure to test |
|---|---|
| Home services | Visible symptom → diagnosis → demonstrated fix → local proof → estimate CTA |
| Fitness and education | Human moment → teaching mechanism → observable progress → invitation |
| Professional services | Expensive mistake → proprietary process → case proof → consultation |
| Retail and restaurant | Product ritual → sensory proof or reaction → genuine offer → visit/order CTA |
| Health and beauty | Concern → demonstration → realistic proof → risk reversal → consultation |

## Patterns to reject

- unsupported medical, developmental, causal, or exclusivity claims;
- fear-heavy deterioration language;
- fake scarcity, fake shutdowns, resetting deadlines, or permanent discounts;
- fabricated testimonials or AI-generated customers presented as real;
- long AI-song ads as a core Joao format;
- broad e-commerce creative volume that overwhelms a geographically limited budget;
- judging success from views, library size, or apparent longevity alone;
- sending audience-specific ads to generic pages with weak message match.

## Build-time resurfacing checklist

Before writing or approving a Joao ad campaign, explicitly revisit this entry and answer:

- [ ] What single audience, offer, landing page, and conversion event does this campaign use?
- [ ] What are the three genuinely different hook families?
- [ ] Which concepts need short and authority-length cuts?
- [ ] What real footage or proof demonstrates Joao’s mechanism?
- [ ] What proof and objection retargeting ads are included?
- [ ] Does each ad match its page hero, mechanism, location, and CTA?
- [ ] Are all stories, names, minors, footage, quotes, schedules, prices, and claims approved?
- [ ] Are UTMs and qualified-lead outcome reporting defined?
- [ ] Are success decisions based on qualified leads, attendance, enrollment, and bookings rather than vanity metrics?
