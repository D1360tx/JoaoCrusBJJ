## Core mechanism

The page reframes beginner frustration as a curriculum problem rather than a talent or memory problem:

> Most people are taught Jiu-Jitsu as a pile of moves.

> YOU DON'T NEED MORE MOVES. YOU NEED FEWER THINGS.

The course sells a small set of principles taught in sequence. The named principles include base, connection, weight distribution, strategy, first thoughts, breath, and resilience. The promised shift is from memorizing disconnected techniques to understanding why movements work.
