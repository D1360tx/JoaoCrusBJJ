## Claim and positioning cautions

- The father’s start age, occupation, current age, and expected promotion are advertiser-stated.
- “Time-collapse your learning curve,” “way better, way faster,” and promotion timing are aggressive claims without training-frequency or promotion-standard context.
- “Your brain might be burnt out,” “your body isn’t what it used to be,” and “combat those young bucks” can create age-shame or antagonism.
- “Be careful who you train with” is valuable but incomplete safety guidance. A local academy should explain partner matching, pace, coach supervision, and adaptation rather than only warning about risk.
