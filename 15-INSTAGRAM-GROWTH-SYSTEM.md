## 1. Strategic decision

### Apply

- Use AI to structure audience research, content briefs, Reel scripts, optimization, repurposing, and performance reviews.
- Start every content request with Joao's verified facts, audience, business objective, available visual proof, offer, and CTA.
- Convert successful prompts into reusable templates and a governed production workflow.
- Use Instagram as a local trust and conversion channel, not merely an engagement scoreboard.
- Integrate Instagram with the YouTube content system so one source recording can serve both channels without creating two disconnected calendars.

### Do not apply

- Do not paste generic “viral content” prompts into Claude and publish the first output.
- Do not treat AI-generated audience fears or motivations as research facts.
- Do not publish fifty interchangeable ideas merely because a prompt requested volume.
- Do not imitate exaggerated hooks that conflict with Joao's calm authority.
- Do not use follower growth as the primary success measure.
- Do not automate posting, replies, DMs, claims, or paid promotion without human approval.

### Core principle

> **The prompt is not the strategy. Joao's positioning, proof, offers, and real audience response are the strategy. AI helps operate it consistently.**

## 6. Joao-specific AI modules

These replace the thread's generic prompts. Each module should use verified project context and produce structured drafts for review.

### Module A: Strategy brief

**Purpose:** Define one audience, one business outcome, content pillars, offer path, and measurement plan.

**Required inputs:**

- Audience segment
- Current business priority
- Verified offer and schedule
- Available proof and footage
- Approved positioning
- Constraints and claim guardrails
- Performance baseline

**Required output:**

- Audience problem
- Joao's credible angle
- Three to five content themes
- Appropriate formats
- CTA map
- Risks and missing evidence
- Success metrics

### Module B: Audience research engine

Use three evidence layers:

1. **Verified:** parent interviews, intake calls, reviews, comments, DMs, search queries, form questions, and Joao's firsthand observations
2. **Supported hypothesis:** recurring patterns from competitor ads or relevant market research
3. **Unknown:** AI-generated possibilities requiring validation

Required output fields:

- Question or concern
- Evidence source
- Awareness stage
- Emotional context
- Objection
- Desired outcome
- Safe content angle
- Suitable offer

AI must never relabel hypotheses as customer evidence.

### Module C: Content idea engine

Generate a prioritized test matrix rather than a flat list of fifty ideas.

Every idea must include:

- Series
- Audience
- One-sentence hook
- Teaching point
- Real visual proof required
- CTA
- Claim or consent risk
- Effort estimate
- Reuse potential

Rank ideas by business relevance, proof availability, distinctiveness, and production feasibility.

### Module D: Hook and Reel builder

For one approved idea, create:

- Three hook options with different mechanisms
- Spoken script in Joao's natural voice
- Shot list using real footage
- On-screen text
- Pattern interruption or demonstration moment
- CTA matched to the topic
- Shorter cut option
- Caption and cover title
- Claims/accuracy checklist

Hooks should create curiosity or relevance without manufacturing fear or promising outcomes.

### Module E: Content optimizer

Review a draft or existing post for:

- Weak opening
- Generic language
- Missing audience specificity
- Unsupported claims
- Excess explanation before the proof
- Poor visual-to-script match
- Weak or mismatched CTA
- Missing location/program clarity
- Caption readability

Return an improved version plus a concise explanation of what changed. Preserve the core teaching point and Joao's tone.

### Module F: Sales-content generator

Use only approved offers:

- Age-3/kids class match
- Adult cohort or interest path based on verified schedule
- Private game assessment
- Storybook/lead magnet
- Future coach-education waitlist after validation

Each sales concept should lead with a real problem or observable moment, teach something useful, present proof, and explain the next step. Do not invent rates, scarcity, bonuses, guarantees, or student outcomes.

### Module G: Repurposing engine

Convert one approved source into a controlled package:

- Instagram Reel
- Carousel outline
- Caption
- Stories sequence
- YouTube Short or long-form section
- Beehiiv excerpt
- Website FAQ/article excerpt
- Paid-ad concept when appropriate

Adapt the structure for each format instead of copying identical text. Publishing every derivative is optional.

### Module H: Performance and planning loop

Use actual Instagram Insights and conversion data to answer:

- Which hooks reached non-followers?
- Which topics generated saves, shares, profile visits, or questions?
- Which series produced tracked website actions or inquiries?
- Where did viewers leave the video?
- Which format deserves another test?
- Which content attracted the wrong audience?
- What should be repeated, revised, paused, or promoted?

The loop proposes the next content batch. A human approves it.

## 7. Integrated production workflow

### Stage 1: Source capture

Use approximately one useful instructional anchor per week, consistent with Joao's existing cadence preference. Supplement it with authentic academy proof when available rather than forcing a high-volume schedule.

### Stage 2: Intake and transcription

Record the source file, transcript, participants, consent, location, audience, offer, objective, and available landing page.

### Stage 3: Evidence and claims review

Check names, lineage, schedules, ages, prices, locations, testimonials, developmental language, and minor consent.

### Stage 4: Format selection

Choose the smallest number of formats that serve the idea and objective. Not every lesson needs a Reel, carousel, Stories sequence, email, and ad.

### Stage 5: Packaging

Prepare hooks, script, shot list, cover, caption, CTA, alt text when practical, and tracked destination.

### Stage 6: Human approval

Joao or Diego approves technical accuracy, tone, footage, claims, consent, offer, CTA, and final edit.

### Stage 7: Publishing and engagement

Publish the approved asset. Handle comments and DMs as real conversations. Automation may triage or draft replies but must not impersonate Joao without approval.

### Stage 8: Measurement and learning

Record native Instagram metrics and downstream conversion events. Feed the results into the next planning cycle.

## 10. Measurement framework

### Native Instagram metrics to capture

- Accounts reached
- Follower versus non-follower reach
- Plays and watch time
- Opening retention when available
- Completion or average watch percentage when available
- Saves and shares
- Meaningful comments and questions
- Profile activity
- Follows attributed to the post
- Link taps
- Story exits, replies, and sticker taps

### Business metrics

- Class-match starts and completions
- Private-coaching inquiries
- Booked actions
- Email/lead-magnet opt-ins
- Qualified DMs routed into an owned lead process
- Assisted conversions

### Diagnostic rules

- High views with low profile or destination activity indicate weak audience/offer alignment.
- High saves with weak reach may indicate valuable content with poor packaging.
- Strong profile activity with weak form completion indicates profile, link-hub, offer, or landing-page friction.
- High engagement from coaches but little local response may signal audience dilution.
- Native Insights are required before concluding that the existing 8,000 followers are inactive, irrelevant, or artificial.

## 11. 60-day pilot structure

### Days 1–7: Baseline and setup

- Export native Instagram Insights
- Audit follower geography and active times
- Review top and bottom posts by reach, watch time, shares, saves, profile actions, and downstream clicks
- Confirm account access, bio, link destination, pinned posts, and Highlights
- Create intake, consent, Reel, carousel, caption, CTA, and analytics templates
- Select the first four weekly anchors

### Days 8–30: Controlled publishing

- Publish approximately one useful instructional anchor per week
- Test the four primary franchises
- Add real Stories proof when available
- Use one clear CTA per asset
- Record performance and viewer questions
- Avoid major cadence increases while rebuilding signal quality

### Days 31–60: Evidence-based iteration

- Repeat the strongest audience/topic/hook combinations
- Repackage valuable low-reach content with improved hooks or covers
- Pause formats that attract irrelevant audiences
- Test one offer-led asset only after the educational series establishes message fit
- Decide whether to maintain, expand, or narrow the cadence

## 12. Profile and conversion prerequisites

Before scaling content volume:

- Confirm the canonical website destination
- Ensure GA4 and Meta event tracking are working
- Preserve UTMs through forms and booking
- Publish accurate program, schedule, location, and private-coaching pages
- Make mobile calls, texts, maps, and forms functional
- Reconcile the paid/deposit trial offer and rates before sales posts
- Establish response ownership for comments, DMs, calls, and forms

More content sent into an unclear or untracked path will amplify leakage rather than solve it.

## 13. Automation boundaries

### Safe to automate first

- Transcription
- Content tagging
- Audience/question clustering
- Hook and script drafts
- Shot lists and edit briefs
- Caption and carousel drafts
- CTA/UTM preparation
- Calendar organization
- Analytics collection and comparison
- Draft replies for review

### Approval required

- Publishing and scheduling
- Use of minors' footage
- Replies sent as Joao
- DM sequences or lead outreach
- Paid promotion
- Offers, prices, guarantees, urgency, and bonuses
- Developmental, confidence, anti-bullying, safety, belt, or competition claims

### Technical safeguards

- Preserve original media
- Work from sandboxed copies
- Log drafts and approvals
- Cap API spending and batch size
- Do not grant experimental agents production publishing credentials
- Keep a manual kill switch for every scheduled or looping process
